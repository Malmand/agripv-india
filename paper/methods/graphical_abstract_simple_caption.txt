Graphical abstract (simple version). Left: the question. Centre: a light model and the DSSAT crop model give the harvest under the panels as a percentage of the open-field harvest for increasing panel cover (ground coverage ratio, the share of land covered by modules); the curve is schematic. The dot marks the largest panel cover that keeps at least 80% of the open-field harvest. Right: that largest panel cover for east-west panels under 1995–2014 climate, robust case, in which each cell, crop and GCM takes the smaller panel cover of the calibrated and recommended nitrogen runs. For each 0.25° cell and crop it is the median over five GCMs; the map shows the mean of the four crops weighted by eligible area (MapSPAM harvested area on the share of the cell with slope ≤ 5%), and cells with less than 1000 ha are blank. The cereal value is the eligible-area-weighted mean over all wheat and pearl millet cells, the legume value over all chickpea and groundnut cells. The headline is the crops-combined capacity (double-cropped land counted once), median of the five national totals. The electricity multiple is its annual generation divided by India's 2025–26 electricity requirement (1,707 TWh; CEA Load Generation Balance Report 2026-27). The 2050 line gives the change in capacity by 2041–2060 under SSP5-8.5 against 1995–2014 (five-GCM median, calibrated nitrogen). State and union-territory boundaries: DataMeet India community, States/Admin2 (https://github.com/datameet/maps, CC BY 4.0).
